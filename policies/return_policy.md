# Return Policy

## Return Window

Most products may be returned within 30 calendar days after delivery. A return authorization must be requested before the item is sent back.

## Condition Requirements

Returned items must be unused, clean, and in resalable condition. A brief fit check is allowed for apparel and footwear.

## Size-Related Returns

Apparel or footwear with a size or fit issue may be returned or exchanged within the return window after an indoor try-on. Worn, washed, or visibly soiled items are not eligible.

## Packaging and Accessories

Original packaging, manuals, tags, and included accessories must be returned when supplied. Missing components may reduce the refund by their documented replacement cost.

## Exclusions

Personalized goods, opened hygiene-sensitive products, gift cards, and final-sale items cannot be returned unless they arrived defective or damaged.
