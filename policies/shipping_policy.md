# Shipping Policy

## Standard Shipping

Standard orders are processed within 1 to 2 business days and normally arrive 3 to 5 business days after dispatch. Delivery estimates exclude weekends and public holidays.

## Expedited Shipping

Expedited orders placed before 1:00 p.m. local warehouse time normally dispatch the same business day. Expedited delivery usually takes 1 to 2 business days after dispatch.

## Tracking

A tracking link is sent when the carrier accepts the parcel. Tracking may take up to 24 hours to show its first scan.

## Lost or Damaged Shipments

A shipment with no tracking movement for 7 calendar days after the expected delivery date may be treated as lost. Customers should report a lost parcel or shipping damage within 7 calendar days; we will investigate and provide a replacement or refund when the claim is confirmed.

## Address Changes

The delivery address can be changed only before the order enters packing. After dispatch, address changes depend on the carrier and are not guaranteed.
