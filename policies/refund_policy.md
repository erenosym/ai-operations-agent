# Refund Policy

## Eligibility

Customers may request a refund within 30 calendar days after delivery. The order number and a short explanation of the issue are required.

## Defective or Damaged Items

Items that arrive defective or damaged are eligible for a full refund, including standard shipping charges. The customer should provide a photo of the damage when reasonably possible; return shipping is provided at no cost.

## Refund Method

Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method. Store credit may be offered only when the original method is unavailable and the customer agrees.

## Refund Processing

Approved refunds are submitted within 5 business days after inspection or, when no return is required, after approval. A bank may need an additional 3 to 7 business days to post the credit.

## Exclusions

Gift cards, final-sale items, and damage caused by misuse are non-refundable. Shipping upgrades are not refunded unless the shipment was late because of our carrier or fulfillment error.
